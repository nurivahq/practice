About Language Whispers

Language learning should feel personal.

LW

Language Whispers was created for learners who want more than simply attending a class. We believe progress comes from having someone who listens to you, corrects you, guides you, and helps you keep going.

That is why our lessons are one-to-one. You get the teacher's full attention, the freedom to ask questions, and space to practice without feeling lost in a crowd.

Lessons take place online through WhatsApp, Telegram, or Zoom. Choose a time that works for you and learn from wherever you are. A Language Whispers mobile app is also coming soon.
